# Loot drops — short design (approved by Drei 2026-11-24, "Go ahead")

**Why:** kills should feel rewarding and give the crew something to scramble for.

**What:** extra loot on top of the gold every kill already pays the crew.
- Normal monster, rewarded kill: coin pile 35% (1–2x that monster's gold roll), heart 12% (heals 20% max HP on touch, never bagged), potion 6%, feather 2%. At most one drop per kill.
- Boss: always 5–8 coin piles + 1 potion, scattered around the body.
- No drops from summoned minions or noReward kills.
- Loot lies on the floor 12 s (blinks the last 3 s), is cleared when the room changes.
- First-come, first-served (Drei's call): first living hero within reach takes it all. Downed heroes can't pick up.
- No bag limit exists in the game, so there is no "bag full" rule (my call; nothing to check against).
- Client: pixel icons (coin/potion/feather + new heart), pop-out bounce, "+N gold" / "+1 potion" float text for the picker, pickup sound.

**Approaches considered:** shared coins (Recommended at the time) vs everything-to-picker vs everything-shared. Drei picked everything-to-picker ("paunahan").
